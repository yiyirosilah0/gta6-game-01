Meridian Heat is a complete, original, top-down open-world crime game in the sun-soaked city of Meridian Bay. Walk and drive a living city with traffic and pedestrians, steal cars, outrun the police under a five-star wanted system, fight on foot, and work a three-mission story — Hot Wheels, The Package, Lose the Heat — plus repeatable courier jobs for cash. A HUD with health, cash, wanted stars and a live minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, jingles), a save that carries your cash and story progress, and a title screen, pause and game-over flow. It runs offline in a desktop browser from its own static files, no network, no library.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
